## 1. The three bad blocks, identified by measurement

The reveal instrument now reports **local readability separately from macro reveal** (§15). Run
against the shipped floor it names all three of the human's complaints, independently:

| Standing in | Local readability |
|---|---|
| south lane | **THE ENVOY ITSELF IS COVERED** |
| hall | **THE ENVOY ITSELF IS COVERED** |
| junction | **THE ENVOY ITSELF IS COVERED** |
| everywhere else | clear, or 4% of the ring |

Those are exactly the spike-entry block, the screenshot's foreground wall, and the final-gate
block. And they are precisely the three masses whose **only** job was covering — which is what
§5 says not to author.

> Confirmed as a general property, not three coincidences: **every mass that exists only to hide
> something ended up covering the player.** A mass that also does gameplay work sits where the
> room wants it, and rooms do not want walls on top of the player.

## 2. The lateral-accumulation experiment, and what it cost

I costed the obvious reading of §4 — translate the late chapter **east** so displacement
accumulates instead of returning to the centreline — with the three blocks removed.

**It fixes the early floor and breaks the late one:**

| From | Shipped | Amended |
|---|---|---|
| drop | 5 in view, 3 solved | **4 in view, 3 solved** — better |
| landing | 3 in view | **2 in view** — better |
| south lane | 5 in view, 1 solved | **6 in view, 5 SOLVED** — much worse |
| hall | 5 in view, 2 solved | **6 in view, 5 SOLVED**, terrace at 60% | 
| puzzle bay | 5 in view, 3 solved | **5 in view, 5 SOLVED** |

**Cause:** a late chapter strung out along one line — lane → hall → bay → junction → terrace,
each roughly below the last — is a corridor the camera reads end to end, and the near walls that
had been hiding those relationships were the ones removed.

**So the pure translation trades the human's foreground complaint for the original
see-everything complaint.** Reporting it rather than shipping it.

## 3. What I propose instead

**Re-place the near walls rather than delete them, and accumulate laterally more modestly.**

The objection was never to near walls. It was to walls **that cover the player**. A near wall
belonging to a room, offset so the player walks *beside* it rather than *under* it, occludes just
as well and reads as architecture.

Three changes, in priority order (§5 — topology first, blocks last):

1. **Push the Court/Vault chapter further east and the Hall chapter further west**, so the two
   halves occupy genuinely different regions. Accumulation comes from *where the chapters are*,
   not from a monotonic sweep.
2. **Re-place the three offenders**, each moved off the walking line:
   - *spike-entry block* → moved west, clear of the lane's mouth, so the pads and their state
     are fully visible on approach and the commitment edge is uncluttered.
   - *hall near wall* → shortened and moved to the Hall's **west** end, hiding the puzzle bay
     from the Court without standing over the player crossing the Hall.
   - *final-gate block* → **deleted**, and its reveal job handed to the Junction's approach angle.
     It had no gameplay role, which is the test §8 sets.
3. **Only then** check whether anything is still over-exposed.

## 4. Spike grace

Measured traversal is **99 ticks**. The current 124 (1.25×) was human-falsified as "barely
succeeds".

**Proposed: 150 ticks (≈1.5×).** That buys ~1.7 s of reading and reaction on top of the walk,
which matches the intent — *the challenge is recognising the opening and committing, not
executing inside a near-frame-perfect budget.* Human tunes by feel after.

## 5. Large-space content identities (§10–12)

Not uniform distribution — each space gets one job:

| Space | Identity | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| **Landing** | first contact, open | ambient pair; no props — the floor's introduction stays legible |
| **West Hall** | quiet connector | vegetation cluster only; a breather with something to look at |
| **Court** | **landmark, open movement** | a central structure to fight around, asymmetric obstacle groups at its edges, ambient pair. Deliberately keeps open ground — it is a Court, not a maze |
| **Vault** | optional destination | empty, honestly; payoff still blocked on reward vocabulary |
| **South lane** | **hazard focus** | spikes and nothing else. One idea, cleanly read |
| **Hall** | **integrated encounter** | Watcher behind rubble, Fangs round the masses — unchanged, it works |
| **Puzzle bay** | **switch puzzle** | the toggle and its doors; one search prop. No combat — the puzzle is the beat |
| **Junction** | staged fight, open | enemy group with two approach lanes |
| **Terrace** | arrival | exit plate; nothing to distract from it |

**Two spaces get no combat at all** (west hall, puzzle bay) and one gets no props (south lane).
That variation *is* the pacing.
