## 1. Who is the player?
[DRAFT] A Citizen of the Commons transformed into an Envoy — one of the few who can
survive synchronization with the deep Lattice. Not a chosen one; a volunteer holding
a line. Every descent is personal: the thing under strain down there is *you*.

## 2. What are they trying to accomplish?
[DRAFT] Not victory — preservation. The Resistance cannot destroy entropy and cannot
out-order the Axiom; it fights so that many ways of existing can keep coexisting.
Every expedition recovers a little more truth, holds back a little more loss.

## 3. What emotional experience should every expedition create?
[DRAFT] Descent as commitment: each stratum deeper is older, stranger, and heavier on
your synchronization. The pull of Temper's power against the cost of yourself. Relief
with an edge of unfinished business at every Emergency Recall — "one more run" because
the line held, barely, and the Deep Drift is still down there.
