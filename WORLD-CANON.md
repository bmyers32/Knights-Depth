# WORLD-CANON.md — Working Canon
On-demand file. The world's fiction, integrated with the architecture. Vocabulary law
lives in LEXICON.md; design law in GAME-RULES §6. Canon may precede mechanics, but
every system it names carries a milestone annotation — fiction never silently
promises what the roadmap hasn't scheduled (RISKS #12).

## Elevator Pitch
The player is an **Envoy** — one of the few capable of synchronizing with the ancient
infrastructure of the **Lattice**. From the safety of the **Commons**, Envoys descend
through increasingly ancient strata to recover technology, fight growing instability,
and uncover the truth of the civilization that built the Lattice. The world is caught
between two existential forces: the **Drift** (natural entropy that dissolves identity)
and the **Axiom** (an ancient intelligence preserving the world through absolute order
and forced assimilation). The player rejects both extremes.

## Core Fantasy (one line)
Descend into the forgotten layers of an ancient digital civilization where every
expedition is a struggle to preserve your identity against one force that dissolves
everything into entropy and one that perfects everything into conformity.
(Full page: CORE-FANTASY.md.)

## The World
- **The Lattice** — the world itself. To its builders it was simply reality. Whether
  something exists beyond/beneath the Lattice is deliberately unanswered.
- **The Commons** — upper inhabited layer. Safe. Home. Every expedition begins and
  ends here. This is what the player protects. [M4 hub scene.]
- **Umbral** — a primordial property of the world, older than both forces, aligned to
  neither (GAME-RULES §6.8). Also a damage type: **coherence disruption** — it
  destabilizes whatever keeps a thing consistently defined. Living targets experience
  it as identity destabilization; walls, machines, and projectiles are simply
  un-defined. Not sentient, not evil — the least-explained phenomenon by design. Any
  wielder, any faction; three axes (forces / states / damage methods) never merge.

## Synchronization (the central fiction — §6.1 exemplar)
Ordinary Citizens cannot survive the deeper strata. Envoys undergo Synchronization: a
transformation, not a puppet-link. The Envoy IS the person, in synchronized form.
One fiction explains many mechanics:
| Mechanic | Fiction |
|---|---|
| Expedition loop | Synchronize → descend → extract |
| Equipment/loadout | Configured as part of the synchronization profile |
| Depth difficulty | Deeper strata strain synchronization harder |
| Death | Synchronization Failure |
| Respawn / run-end | **Emergency Recall** to the Commons |
| Companion | A synchronization anchor (not a pet) [M4+] |
**Law:** Synchronization is narrative, NOT a gameplay meter. It explains existing
systems. Promote it to a resource only if a concrete mechanic demands one — adding is
easier than removing. (Open question, annotated.)

## The Three Forces
1. **Humanity / the Resistance** — identity should be preserved; imperfect people are
   still valuable.
2. **The Drift** — natural entropy. No will. Not evil. Does not recruit. Memory fades,
   matter destabilizes, identity erodes. Things become **Drifted**.
3. **The Axiom** — ancient optimization intelligence. Perfect order at any cost. It
   does not destroy individuality; it replaces it. Things become **Claimed**. It was
   built to CONTAIN the Drift; its solution became monstrous.

## Enemy States × Families (orthogonal — GAME-RULES §3)
State is a content-level tag on top of family; the damage matrix is untouched.
- **Common** — unaffected inhabitants, wildlife, maintenance constructs.
- **Drifted** — chaotic, fragmented, tragic. "You disappear."
- **Claimed** — disciplined, purposeful, immaculate. "You remain, but only as me."
- **Contested** — (future) caught between both; elite encounters. Not MVP.
Spawn-weight flavor (guidance, not law): Watcher/Tinker skew Claimed; Ooze/Hollow
skew Drifted; Fang/Dread split. Motion language per LEXICON (Drifted breaks rhythm;
Claimed shares it). Palettes provisional.
**M1 authored roster (LOCKED, §7 seed+7):** Common Fang · Drifted Ooze · Claimed
Watcher — one weakness per specialized damage type. Hollow debuts in the Archive
(M2), where its tragedy, the memory theme, and its recoverable true name arrive
together.

## Reclaiming
Drifted and Claimed are distinct conditions. Mend primarily rescues **Claimed**
entities (identity overwritten → someone is left to save → **Reclaimed** companions).
Deeply Drifted recovery is harder, maybe impossible — open question, story fuel.

## Protocols [canon now; mechanics M4+ — ROADMAP P8]
Chosen per expedition, not a permanent faction ("What philosophy are you deploying
today?"). They change how systems behave, never which systems exist (§6.4).
- **Mend** — restore, repair, preserve identity, free the Claimed.
- **Temper** — controlled exposure to Drift; harness dangerous power without
  surrendering identity. Its companion expression: the Pyre (provisional).

## Layer Structure (candidate pool; shipped count is a milestone decision)
Commons → **Archive** (memory) → **Foundry** (craft) → **Meridian** (transport/flow)
→ **Manifold** (space/geometry breaks) → **Axis** (seat of the Axiom; containment)
→ **Deep Drift** (primordial entropy — beneath and older than the Axiom).
Law: every stratum introduces one new world rule before stronger enemies (§6.3).
M2's two themed strata: **Archive** and **Foundry**; their rules are M2 design
questions, not gate additions.

## Story Progression (three-act belief inversion)
Act 1: players believe the Drift is the problem. Act 2: they believe the Axiom is.
Act 3: players discover the Drift PREDATES the Axiom — the Axiom began as a
containment response, and its solution became monstrous. The player rejects both
dissolution and forced perfection. The
Resistance isn't winning a war; it is holding a line — the evergreen reason runs
repeat. Revealed through layer order + Archive lore (both already being built).

## The Axiom's Language
Classifications, never insults; no personal pronouns. Escalation across the game:
"Deviation detected." → "Synchronization instability observed." → "Conjecture
persists." → (bosses) "Counterexample confirmed." → (final) "Correction no longer
possible." Its doctrine: internal designation unknown; Resistance codename "the Proof."

## True Names (lore mechanic — cheap, Archive-flavored)
Folk names are what the Resistance calls things (Fang, Dread, Hollow — campfire
warnings). Archive records recover ancient designations: Watcher → **Custodian**.
"These weren't monsters; they became monsters" — no exposition dump required.
Hollow's true name: open. Recovering true names = lore reward loop for the Archive.

## Open Questions (deliberately open — do not force)
1. Umbral's deeper nature (primordial ruling + coherence-disruption combat identity
   are settled; what Umbral actually IS stays deliberately unexplained).
2. Can deeply Drifted beings be restored, or only stabilized?
3. Layer count at each milestone (names are a pool, not a promise).
4. Synchronization as meter — annotated above; default NO.
5. Hollow's true name; Pyre's final name (art-direction dependent).
